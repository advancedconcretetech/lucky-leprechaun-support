Common Questions

What kind of game is this?
Lucky Leprechaun Gold Hunt is a slot-style game intended for entertainment. It does not offer real-money gambling or cash prizes.

How can I manage notifications?
You can manage the game's notification permissions in your iPhone or iPad Settings under Notifications.

The game isn't working correctly. What should I do?
Try closing and reopening the app, checking for updates in the App Store, and restarting your device. If the problem continues, email our support team.
