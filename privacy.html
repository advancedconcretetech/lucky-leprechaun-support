SupportPrivacy Policy

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ADVANCED CONCRETE TECHNOLOGIES, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Lucky Leprechaun Gold Hunt mobile application (the "App"). If you have questions, contact us at info@advancedconcrete.mom.

2. Information We May Process

When you use the App, limited technical information may be processed to operate the App and provide push notifications. Depending on your device, permissions, and the configuration of our notification provider, this may include a push notification token, app or device identifiers, device and operating-system details, notification subscription status, and technical information about notification delivery or interactions.

If you contact us for support, we process the email address and information you choose to provide, such as a description of the problem and optional screenshots.

3. Push Notifications and OneSignal

We use OneSignal as a service provider to help deliver and manage push notifications. OneSignal may process the technical information needed to register devices for notifications, deliver messages, and measure notification delivery or engagement, depending on the features enabled in the App. For details about OneSignal's practices, see OneSignal's Privacy Policy.

You can allow or decline notification permission when prompted by iOS. You can also change notification permissions at any time in your device's Settings. Declining notifications does not prevent you from using the App's core gameplay.

4. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the App.
  • To deliver and manage notifications when permitted.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To protect the reliability and security of the App.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

We may share limited technical information with service providers, including OneSignal, to perform the functions described above. We do not sell personal information. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or to protect legal rights.

6. Retention and Security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including support, operational, and legal needs. Service providers may apply their own retention periods. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may disable notifications in iOS Settings. To ask questions or request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with a support request or notification subscription, contact info@advancedconcrete.mom. We will respond as required by applicable law and may need information to verify a request.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is a simulated slot-style game. It is not specifically directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

9. International Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we take steps intended to ensure appropriate protections under applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the effective date.
